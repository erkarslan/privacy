Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 5, 2026

This privacy policy applies to all apps developed by Erkan Arslan (developer account: Renkasoft). It explains how we handle your data.

Data Collection

Our apps do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All data (tasks, settings, statistics, etc.) is stored locally on your device only. No data is ever sent to our servers or any third party by us.

Third-Party Services

Our apps may use the following third-party services solely for payment processing:

  • Apple App Store (iOS) — for processing in-app purchases. Apple may collect payment information as per their own privacy policy.
  • Google Play Billing (Android) — for processing in-app purchases. Google may collect payment information as per their own privacy policy.

We do not share any data with any other third parties.

Permissions

Some apps may request device permissions (such as notifications or alarms) solely to provide core functionality. These permissions are never used to collect data.

Data Storage & Retention

All app data is stored locally on your device. Data is retained until you delete the app or manually clear its data. We have no access to this data and cannot retrieve or delete it on your behalf.

Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because we do not collect any personal data, there is no data held by us to access, correct, export, or delete. If you wish to remove all app data, simply uninstall the app from your device.

Children's Privacy

Our apps do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. Since no data is collected at all, the apps are safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
