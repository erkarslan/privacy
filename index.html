Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This privacy policy applies to all mobile applications published by Renkasoft on Google Play and the Apple App Store (“we”, “our”, “the apps”). It explains what information the apps handle, why, and the choices you have.

In short: we have no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Most app data stays on your device. Apps with AI features send only the content you choose to submit, to generate a response, and we do not keep that content on our servers.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

Content you create in the apps, such as tasks, notes, achievements, meditation sessions, recipes, your ingredient list, settings and preferences, is stored locally on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers, except for the specific AI requests described in section 3. Documents you open to read aloud or view are processed on your device and are not uploaded.

2. Anonymous App Identifier

Apps that offer AI features or cloud-verified subscriptions create an anonymous identifier through Google Firebase Authentication. No name, email address, phone number or other contact detail is requested or linked to it. We use this identifier only to:

  • enforce monthly usage limits for AI features (a request counter per month);
  • record your subscription status (Pro / Free), its expiry date, the product ID and the store transaction ID, so that purchases can be verified, restored and refunded correctly;
  • record that you accepted the AI-services notice (acceptance, policy version and date), where an app asks for this consent.

3. AI Features

Some apps offer optional AI features, such as monthly progress reports, recipe suggestions or reading the label on a bottle or bag from a photo. When you use these features:

  • The data needed for that request is sent over an encrypted connection to our server (Google Cloud / Firebase) and forwarded to Anthropic (Claude API) to generate a response. Depending on the feature, this can be the text entries and categories you have logged, the ingredients and preferences you selected, or a photo you took or picked for scanning.
  • Some apps may generate an illustrative image for a recipe using Replicate. Only the recipe description is sent; no personal data or photos are included.
  • We do not store your AI request content or photos on our servers. Only the usage counter described in section 2 is kept. Short technical logs, such as a request status or recognized product name, may be kept temporarily for error diagnosis.
  • AI output may be inaccurate. Please do not enter sensitive personal, financial or health information into the apps.

AI features are optional. The rest of each app works without them.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, App Check, Remote Config): anonymous identifier, usage limits, subscription status, and app integrity checks (Google Play Integrity / Apple App Attest), which verify that requests come from a genuine copy of the app. — Firebase Privacy
  • Anthropic (Claude API): processes AI requests. — Anthropic Privacy Policy
  • Replicate: AI image generation in some apps. — Replicate Privacy Policy
  • Google Play / Apple App Store: payment processing for in-app purchases. — Google Privacy Policy, Apple Privacy Policy

We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and we do not sell or share your data for advertising.

5. Purchases

Some apps offer optional in-app purchases or subscriptions. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your payment details. Purchases are platform-specific and do not transfer between iOS and Android. Sign in with the same store account and use “Restore purchases” to unlock previously bought features on your other devices.

6. Permissions

Each app requests only the permissions it needs for its features:

  • Notifications, exact alarms, run at startup, vibration, wake lock: reminders, timers and scheduled notifications, including after a device restart.
  • Camera and Photos / Media: only when you choose to photograph or pick an image, for example to scan a product label.
  • Files: only documents you explicitly select to open in the app.
  • Internet: AI features, in-app purchases, and loading online content.

The apps do not access your contacts, location, microphone or call history. You can revoke any permission at any time in your device settings.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

  • On-device data is removed when you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.
  • Usage counters are kept per month and serve only to enforce limits.
  • Subscription and consent records are kept for as long as needed to provide purchased features, handle refunds and meet legal obligations.

Because the identifier is anonymous, we cannot link records to you personally. To request deletion of server-side records, email us at the address below. If possible, include the app name and the store order or transaction ID, and we will delete the associated records within 30 days.

8. Security

All network communication uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Server access is limited and protected by app integrity checks and access rules.

9. Your Rights (GDPR / KVKK / CCPA)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, or to object to its processing. Since we collect no directly identifying information, most data exists only on your device and is under your control. For any request about server-side records, contact us as described above.

10. Children's Privacy

The apps are not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
