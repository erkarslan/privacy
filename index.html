Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 5, 2026

This privacy policy applies to all mobile applications published under this developer account.

Data Collection

Our apps do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All data is stored locally on your device only and is never sent to any server or third party.

Third-Party Services

Some apps offer optional in-app purchases. Payments are processed by the platform store (Apple App Store or Google Play). We do not handle or store any payment information.

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

We do not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Purchases

In-app purchases are platform-specific and do not transfer between iOS and Android. Sign in with the same App Store or Google Play account and use “Restore purchases” to unlock previously bought features on all your devices. All payments are handled by the respective store; we store no payment information.

Permissions

Some apps may request device permissions (such as notifications or alarms) to provide their core functionality. These permissions are never used to collect personal data.

Data Storage & Retention

All app data is stored locally on your device. To remove all data, simply uninstall the app. We have no access to your data and cannot retrieve or delete it on your behalf.

Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Since no personal data is collected by us, there is nothing to access, correct, export, or delete on our end. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

Children's Privacy

Our apps do not collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
