1. What the App is

The App is a record-keeping and calculation tool for livestock keepers. It estimates live weight from tape measurements, and it stores the herd, weighing, health, breeding and money records that you enter. It is a tool to help you keep your own records. It is not a veterinary, medical, nutritional, financial, tax or legal service.

2. Weights are estimates

Weights calculated from tape measurements are estimates, not certified weights. They can differ from the true weight of an animal (typically by around ±9%, and by more for some animals, breeds, ages or body conditions).

Do not rely on tape estimates for selling or buying by weight, for medication dosing, for show weigh-ins, or for any other purpose where an accurate weight matters. Use a calibrated scale for those purposes. Estimated carcass weights and herd values are rough guides based on the figures you enter, including your own market prices.
