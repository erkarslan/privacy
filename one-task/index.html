One Task – Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 5, 2026

One Task is developed by Renkasoft. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data when you use the One Task app.

Data Collection

One Task does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All data (tasks, settings, statistics) is stored locally on your device only. No data is ever sent to our servers or any third party by us.

Third-Party Services

One Task uses the following third-party services solely for payment processing:

  • Apple App Store (iOS) — for processing in-app purchases. Apple may collect payment information as per their own privacy policy.
  • Google Play Billing (Android) — for processing in-app purchases. Google may collect payment information as per their own privacy policy.

We do not share any data with any other third parties.

Permissions

iOS

  • Notifications — used to remind you about your tasks at scheduled times.

Android

  • Notifications — used to remind you about your tasks at scheduled times.
  • Exact Alarm — used to deliver task reminders at the precise time you set.
  • Receive Boot Completed — used to reschedule notifications after a device restart.

Data Storage & Retention

All app data is stored locally on your device. Data is retained until you delete the app or manually clear its data. We have no access to this data and cannot retrieve or delete it on your behalf.

Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because we do not collect any personal data, there is no data held by us to access, correct, export, or delete. If you wish to remove all app data, simply uninstall One Task from your device.

Children's Privacy

One Task does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. Since no data is collected at all, the app is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
