Privacy Policy

Android apps · Last updated: September 27, 2026

This privacy policy applies to all Android applications published by Renkasoft on Google Play.

Data Collection

Our apps do not collect any personal data. There is no account, no login and no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, location or contacts.

Everything you create in the apps, such as your tasks, notes, entries, sessions and settings, is stored only on your device.

Anonymous Identifier

Some apps create a random, anonymous identifier through Google Firebase. It is not linked to you in any way. It is only used to count monthly usage of AI features and to confirm whether a subscription is active.

AI Features

Some apps offer optional AI features, such as a monthly report, a recipe suggestion or reading a label from a photo. When you use one, only the content needed for that request is sent over an encrypted connection to the AI service (Anthropic Claude). In some apps, a recipe image may also be generated with Replicate. The AI service returns the result to your device. We do not store this content or your photos.

AI features are optional. The apps work without them.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase: anonymous identifier and app integrity check (Play Integrity)
  • Anthropic: AI responses
  • Replicate: AI image generation
  • Google Play: in-app purchases

We do not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Purchases

Some apps offer optional in-app purchases. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We do not see or store any payment information. Use “Restore purchases” with the same Google account to unlock previously bought features.

Permissions

Apps request only the permissions needed for their features, such as notifications and alarms for reminders, or camera and photos when you choose to scan a label. These permissions are never used to collect personal data.

Data Storage & Deletion

Your data stays on your device. To remove it, delete it in the app or uninstall the app. We have no access to your data.

Your Rights (GDPR / KVKK / CCPA)

Since we do not collect personal data, there is nothing for us to access, export or delete on our end. For any questions, contact us below.

Children's Privacy

Our apps do not collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
